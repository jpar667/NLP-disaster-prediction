# Raw competition data

Download from [Kaggle: Natural Language Processing with Disaster Tweets](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/nlp-getting-started/data) and place `train.csv`, `test.csv`, and `sample_submission.csv` here.

The original CSVs are preserved locally and excluded from Git. Training contains 7,613 rows; test and sample submission each contain 3,263 rows. Downloading from Kaggle may require signing in and accepting the competition rules.
